Master Services Agreement

Acme Financial Services Pty Ltd  ·  Northwind Cloud Solutions  ·  Execution copy

It is hereby acknowledged and agreed by and between the parties that the Supplier will provide the cloud services described in Schedule 1 (the “Services”) in accordance with the terms set out below.

4. Service Levels

The Supplier shall use commercially reasonable efforts to ensure the Services are available 99.5% of the time, measured monthly, excluding scheduled maintenance windows notified at least five business days in advance.

The Supplier maintains ISO/IEC 27001 certification across the data centres used to host the Services.

5. Data Residency & Access

Customer Data will be stored and processed within Australia. The Supplier may, where reasonably necessary for support, access Customer Data from locations outside Australia.

6. Limitation of Liability

Each party’s total aggregate liability arising out of or in connection with this Agreement shall not exceed the fees paid by the Customer in the twelve (12) months preceding the event giving rise to the claim.
